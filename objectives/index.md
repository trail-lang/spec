# Objectives Index

This folder defines the foundational intent behind the TRAIL language. Each document
captures one dimension of the “why” and “for what purpose” TRAIL exists.

The Objectives layer establishes the philosophical and strategic basis for the
language specification and the future compiler/runtime ecosystem.

---

## Files in This Folder

### [1-values.md](1-values.md)
Defines the core guiding values of TRAIL.
These are the long-term principles that shape all decisions in the language design.

Values answer the question:
> “What do we believe and optimize for?”

---

### [2-motivation.md](2-motivation.md)
Explains why TRAIL needs to exist and what gaps it fills in modern software
development and AI-assisted system creation.

Motivation answers the question:
> “Why are we building this language at all?”

---

### [3-scope.md](3-scope.md)
Defines the boundaries of what the language includes and what it does not. It also
clarifies TRAIL’s ability to represent:

- solution architecture,
- deployment intent,
- compilation targets,
- concept-based system decomposition.

Scope answers:
> “What problems are in-scope for TRAIL to solve?”

---

### [4-non-goals.md](4-non-goals.md)
Captures what TRAIL will **not** do or become.

This avoids scope creep and keeps the language focused on its purpose.

Non-goals answer:
> “What problems should TRAIL never try to solve?”

---

### [5-use-cases.md](5-use-cases.md)
Defines the concrete workflows, situations and system types TRAIL is intended to
support. These demonstrate how TRAIL is used in practice.

Use-cases answer:
> “What kinds of things should TRAIL be used for?”

---

## Purpose of This Folder

Together, these documents define:

- Why TRAIL exists,
- Who and what it is designed for,
- What direction the language will evolve in,
- And just as importantly, the boundaries that guide its development.

The Objectives layer is **not** the language specification.
It is the guiding philosophy that ensures the spec is consistent, coherent and
future-proof.
